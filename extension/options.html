Connect the extension to your Google Sheet.

Quick setup

  1. Open your Google Sheet → Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Paste in Code.gs from this project and set your own SECRET.
  3. Run setup once, then Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone).
  4. Paste the web app URL and your secret below.

Written answers

Autofill can draft answers to open-ended questions ("Why do you want to work here?", cover letters) from your resume, your profile and the job description, using your Claude subscription. Drafts show in purple.

Needs Claude Code installed and logged in, plus the helper from native/install.sh. Each application's drafts count toward your Claude plan's usage limits.

Saved answers

Answers to other questions, reused when a similar question comes up (filled fields show in blue). Add them here, or answer a form yourself and click Remember my answers on this page in the popup.
